About

Howling Warlocks Productions, LLC is an Atlanta-based film production company team focused on creating untold stories. Founded in 2018, Howling Warlocks Productions has a diverse range of individuals that come from all different backgrounds, generations, and livelihoods. The Production Company specializes in storytelling, short films, commercial shoots, social media, and much more.
